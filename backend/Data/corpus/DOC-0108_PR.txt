Rusanivka Transservice SE today announced a programme of supply agreement across its
regional portfolio.

The programme covers Zaluzhne Gas Compressor Station, Terenivske Transformer Depot,
Substation 'Rakytne', with works scheduled in phases through the coming year.

"Continuity of supply is the measure that matters," said Svitlana Hrytsenko, Chief Executive
Officer of Rusanivka Transservice SE.

Equipment will be sourced through Vitrohin Enerhomerezha JSC under existing framework terms.
