Verkhovyna Industrial Group PJSC confirmed on 29 March that it had completed the acquisition
of Kalynivsky Vodokanal-Servis PJSC from Zoriansky Holding LLC. The transfer took effect the
following day. Prior to completion Kalynivsky Vodokanal-Servis PJSC had been a wholly owned
subsidiary of Zoriansky Holding since 2020. The parties did not disclose the price. Filings
for the 2023 financial year, published in January, still show the previous ownership.
