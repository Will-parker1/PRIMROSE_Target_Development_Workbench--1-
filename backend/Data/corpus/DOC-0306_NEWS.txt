A 34% interest in Kruhliak Enerhoheneratsiya JSC was placed privately last week with
Palewater Assets (Ukraine) LLC, a Ukrainian-registered vehicle incorporated earlier the same
year. The buyer has no published trading history and lists a serviced address in Kyiv.
Kruhliak Enerhoheneratsiya JSC operates Zolotyi Brid Combined Heat and Power Plant, one of
the larger thermal sites in its oblast. Neither the vendor nor Palewater Assets (Ukraine)
would disclose consideration.
